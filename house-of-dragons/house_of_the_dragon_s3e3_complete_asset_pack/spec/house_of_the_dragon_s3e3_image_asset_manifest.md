# 9. Visual consistency rules

- Use one portrait-card template for every character.
- Add **faction**, **role**, and **status** beneath each name.
- Use “missing,” “decoy,” “unknown rider,” and “presumed dead by the court” precisely.
- Never depict the false Daeron as the real prince.
- Never place Tessarion in Team Black’s possession.
- Never label Rhaena as known to Rhaenyra’s government.
- Use black/red-gold accents for Team Black and muted green-gold for Hightower forces.
- Prefer original stylized visuals and diagrams over continuous episode footage.
