# Bolt World: researched synthetic reference candidates

Prepared 2026-09-12. These are original AI-written narrations about documented events, not fictional histories and not transcripts of successful videos.

## Status and boundaries

- Status: editorial candidates; human review, engine validation, audio timing, visual review and audience testing are pending.
- Actual retention, actual engagement, predicted retention and model grades: **unknown**, not 100 and not zero.
- Intended niche: specific pest-control interventions that created additional problems.
- Three five-minute targets, not a universal five-minute requirement. Trim rather than pad if the measured delivery is too short or the middle repeats itself.
- No paid calls, renders, pipeline edits or active-reference replacement are part of this package.
- `reference_corpus.load()` currently reads only root-level JSON files in `fixtures/causal`. This nested directory is deliberately outside that retrieval path. The manifest is an editorial interchange format, NOT a production story or a passed research dossier.
- Do not move these into active retrieval automatically. Existing external references remain separate. Same-model approval is not independent validation.

## What to retrieve from these examples

Retrieve the matched engine's explanation pattern, claim-to-visual relationship, specific callback and distinction between a new fact and a recap. Never retrieve these scripts as sources for another topic's facts. Do not copy their dates, actors, numbers, outcomes or exact prose into a new episode.

Keep `target`, `observed`, `measured` and `audience_result` distinct. Sentence length can be counted; clarity requires review; retention requires viewers. Do not turn editorial preferences into blocking runtime rules.

## Review before promotion

1. A human reviews the cited evidence and all causal wording, including titles and images. The ledgers below are source-linked paraphrases, not machine-verified quotation dossiers.
2. Blind editorial comparison with an external reference: can a new viewer name the actor, goal, intervention, overlooked relationship and outcome? Ask for specific confusing/repeated passages, not just a score.
3. Check the selected engine's real required functions using the saved evidence. Optional endings about later management must not be forced into the original intervention's causal chain.
4. Render a short opening only after the normal approval. Check whether the consequence is visible, the animal is identifiable, and the first answer arrives without a format slogan.
5. Measure narration at natural speed and align every visual anchor. Reuse valid research/audio/assets. A planned beat is not a 30-second still; change visuals when the information changes.
6. After an approved publication, record impressions, CTR with traffic source, 30-second retention, average view duration, average percentage viewed and sample size. Do not infer a niche winner from one small result.

## Deliberately excluded

- Delhi cobra breeding as established history: the corpus's story pattern is useful, but its famous anecdote is not a substitute for historical evidence.
- Lake Victoria's simple cleaner-removal-to-algal-bloom chain: substantive counterevidence requires a different, more carefully sourced account.
- Macquarie's single-cause cat-removal story: disputed attribution is not resolved by adding a hedge to the hook.
- Universal rules that every intervention was unforeseen, achieved its target initially, or produced more of the same pest.

## Source handling

Sources were retrieved on 2026-09-12. Links identify the specific works used. Multiple Vann interviews are accounts from the same historian, not independent archival replications. Government summaries can also contain simplifications; qualifications are preserved in each file. No source images, archival facsimiles or long quotations are licensed by this package. Illustrations must be original and labeled as reconstructions when they could be mistaken for records.
