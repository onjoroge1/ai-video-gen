# What If You Shrunk By Half Every Hour? — ADAPTED for the legless weeble Bolt

Adaptation of `Bolt_Shrink_Every_Hour_Pilot_Spec_v1.md`. Structure, timing, objective (reach the reset
switch before Hour 8), countdown, world resets, hidden consequences, hook C, and the science payoff are all

## Bolt design lock (every start-image prompt, + `reference_paths=[MASCOT_REF]`)
> Bolt is a small rounded matte **white-and-mint-green** robot — a **LEGLESS weeble on a smooth rounded
> pedestal base (NO legs, NO feet, NO humanoid legs)**, two short **stub arms with mitten hands**, a big dark
> glossy visor, **BOTH eyes glowing cyan**, a mint-tipped antenna. Only his SCALE changes across shots;
> identical design, materials, proportions every time.

**Keep white+mint, NOT the spec's "charcoal"** (brand continuity with bolt.png). **Lean into weeble physics:**
he rocks, wobbles, *rights himself* (wobbles but doesn't fall down), bounces, tucks-and-rolls, and gets
launched — these are his verbs. Re-roll any shot that grows legs / dims an eye (~1-in-5 budget).

## The 3 core action rewrites (humanoid → weeble)
| Spec (humanoid) | Adapted (weeble) | Why it's as good or better |
|---|---|---|
| Climbs a chair, plants feet, walks | **Bounce-rolls, hops, wobbles, body-shoves** | Distinctive silhouette-motion; reads instantly |
| "Falls and walks away" | **Tumbles, bounces on his rounded base, rights himself unhurt** | "Wobbles but doesn't fall down" *is* the falls-get-safer beat, and it's on-brand |
| "Steals an RC car / drives" | **Tucks into a ball and ROLLS fast** (round base = natural wheel) | Same advantage beat, no driving rig; funnier; no prop-consistency risk |
| "Plants both feet, pushes the switch" | **Hurls his whole round body at the button; it flexes, then BOUNCES him off** | The bounce-off *is* the absolute-strength failure — visible proof |

## Adapted transcript (VO — 3 lines changed, marked ▲)
> Every hour, you shrink to half your height. By Hour Eight, a fall barely matters—but one water drop can trap you. Bolt has eight hours to reach the reset switch.
> Hour One: child-sized. The door handle is already overhead.
> Hour Two: his phone weighs more than he does. But weight shrinks faster than strength, so he can shove it across a floor vent like a bridge.
> ▲ Hour Three: he falls from the counter—and just bounces. For a moment, small is safe. So he tucks in and rolls.
> Then the house turns hostile. At four inches, carpet becomes a forest. At two inches, the cat attacks. At one inch, a house spider is nearly his size—and a floor vent flings him across the room.
> Hour Eight: Bolt is six millimeters tall, a few milligrams. He reaches the switch—but strong for your size isn't strong. He can't press it.
> Then one drop rolls toward him. At this scale, surface tension is a sticky wall.
> Shrinking makes falls safe and you strong for your size—but ordinary water becomes a cage.
