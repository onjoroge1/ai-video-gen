# What If You Shrunk Every Hour? — Script + Shot-List

**Format:** consequence-machine Short (bespoke) · **Bolt Explains the World**
**Target runtime:** ~40s (value-based — every ~3s a NEW *kind* of consequence; a new WORLD every ~10s)
**Shots:** 12 (1 hook + 11 beats) · vertical 9:16 · NO HUD, minimal burned captions
**Governing transformation:** Bolt shrinks continuously, every hour. Predictable rule, UNPREDICTABLE consequences.
**Retention thesis:** the viewer always knows he'll shrink — but never what problem the next size brings. Dread engine: "where does this end?" Payoff: it never ends — he vanishes.

---

## Data-validation gate (the science is the *cause* under each gag — all textbook-accurate)

| Cue | Real science | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| Falls stop hurting | **Terminal velocity** ∝ size; tiny mass → low terminal velocity → survives impacts | B7 |
| Ant could lift you / bugs turn strong | **Square-cube law**: strength ∝ area (L²), weight ∝ volume (L³) → smaller = relatively stronger | B8 |
| A water drop traps you | **Surface tension** dominates weight at small scale → you can't break the meniscus | B9 |
| You freeze fast (optional swap) | Surface-area-to-volume ratio rises → you lose heat faster | (alt beat) |

Premise (shrinking each hour) is fantasy; the *consequences* are grounded. "You vanish" is a conceptual dread payoff (below cells/atoms you can't exist), not a hard physical claim — keep it poetic, not stated as fact.

## Mini-payoff ledger (every shot earns its place — a fact alone = cut)

Hook shock · 1 joke · 2 new-danger · 3 chase · 4 failure · 5 **reversal** · 6 near-death · 7 **advantage** · 8 danger+science · 9 trap · 10 awe · 11 payoff+loop. → 11 distinct reward types, no repeats back-to-back.

## Predictability audit (at ~1/3, shot 4)
Governing rule is obvious (he keeps shrinking) ✅ — but the *category* of consequence keeps changing: clothes → pet → predator → tools → social → physics → biology → scale. Viewer cannot predict shot 8 from shot 4. **Passes.**

## Visual-proof map (no abstract claim without a physical demo)
Every VO line above resolves to object→action→result (clothes→drowns; phone key→won't press; table→drifts down; water bead→can't escape). No height counter, no "X inches" label — the objects carry the size.

## Production notes

- **Bolt on-model lock (validated in the probe):** bake into EVERY start-image prompt — *"legless rounded pedestal base (NO legs/feet), two short stub arms, BOTH eyes glowing cyan, mint-tipped antenna, matte white + mint."* Re-roll any shot that grows legs or dims the eyes (~1-in-5 budget).
- **Danger-object whitelist only** (big **and** threatening): cat, dog, sneaker-stomp, housefly, ant, water-drop. (Avoid inert big objects — a phone/hand-at-rest reads "cute," per the probe.)
- **Verb + reaction in every prompt** or ~half render passive.
- **Cost mix:** 4 Veo hero shots (3 cat, 6 sneaker, 8 fly, 11 vanish) + 8 Kling ambient. Keeps it affordable vs an all-Veo build (~8 Veo ≈ a day's quota).
- **Captions:** 1–4 words, one orange keyword, bottom-third, drop-shadow, appear ON the action. No HUD.
- **Audio:** narration sped to ~180 wpm (TTS runs slow); rising tension bed; SFX ducked ~8 dB under VO; TP limiter −1.5 dBTP; master to −14 LUFS (target loudnorm I=-13 to land −14). Soft, non-harsh transients. 80 ms fade-in + loop fade.
- **Silent test (ship gate):** mutable — a viewer with sound off must read shrink → world gets bigger/deadlier → he vanishes, from behavior + objects + captions alone.
- **Loop:** shot 11 winks to black → smash-cut to H1 (normal-size Bolt starting to shrink) = clean thematic loop.
