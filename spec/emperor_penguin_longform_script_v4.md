# Why This Penguin Looks Like the Worst Mother

Hook: Why does this emperor penguin look like the worst mother?

1. [setup] (40w) Why does this emperor penguin look like the worst mother? She leaves her only egg with Dad and heads to sea. But if the chick hatches before she gets back, how does a father who hasn't been fishing feed it?

2. [hinge] (10w) There is no nest, yet the egg must stay warm.

3. [mechanism] (28w) So he keeps the egg warm beneath a fold of skin. There, it stays at about thirty-eight degrees, while outside the temperature can fall well below minus thirty-five.

4. [escalation] (15w) He holds the egg on his feet, under a brood pouch, for about two months.

5. [escalation] (26w) Through all this time, he eats nothing. He runs entirely on the fat stored in his body, and some fathers lose nearly half their body mass.

6. [escalation] (25w) Now suppose the chick cracks free before the mother returns. He can feed the chick before the female returns, despite not having eaten for weeks.

7. [reversal] (52w) Out of the ice, the mother returns. But the colony is thousands of birds deep. So she calls, and somewhere in that wall of sound, one voice calls back. His. She takes over feeding the chick with fish and krill she brought back from the sea, and Dad heads out to feed.

8. [verdict] (18w) She didn't leave the egg unprotected. She left it with its father — and came back with dinner.
