# Why This Penguin Looks Like the Worst Mother

Hook: Why does this emperor penguin look like the worst mother?

1. [setup] (40w) Why does this emperor penguin look like the worst mother? She leaves her only egg with Dad and heads to sea. But if the chick hatches before she gets back, how does a father who hasn't been fishing feed it?

2. [hinge] (4w) There is no nest.

3. [mechanism] (23w) So he keeps the egg warm beneath a fold of skin at about 38°C, while the air outside can drop well below −35°C.

4. [escalation] (15w) He holds the egg on his feet, under that brood pouch, for about two months.

5. [escalation] (29w) Through all this time, he eats nothing, running on the fat stored in his body, and by the end some fathers have lost up to half their body mass.

6. [escalation] (32w) Suppose the chick hatches before the mother returns, hungry and needing food. He can feed the chick a milky substance produced in his oesophagus before the female returns. His body makes it.

7. [reversal] (47w) Out of the ice, the mother returns, fat from the sea. But the colony is thousands of birds deep. So she calls, and one voice calls back. His. She takes over feeding the chick food she brought back from the sea, and Dad heads out to feed.

8. [verdict] (18w) She didn't leave the egg unprotected. She left it with its father — and came back with dinner.
