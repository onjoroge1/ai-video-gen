**One question under the whole video:** *Why does she leave her pup on the ice for good?*

**Ending type:** a trade-off with real losses, not an acquittal. The penguin episode acquitted the
mother; this one should not, and the evidence does not.

## 1. Hooks

1. **Why does this harp seal look like the worst mother?** (selected — the series question, then
   the behaviour in the next two sentences)
2. Twelve days of milk. Then she leaves the ice and never comes back.
3. This pup will not eat for six weeks. Its mother just left.
4. She feeds it, fattens it, and abandons it on the ice. On purpose.
5. The richest childhood in the animal kingdom lasts twelve days.

Hook 5 is the runner-up but "richest" is a claim about milk fat that neither read source states,
so it is out. Hook 4 says "abandons", a verdict. Hook 1 keeps the series shape.

Dropped as unsourced on the pages read: the fat content of the milk, "richest milk of any
mammal", the mother fasting while nursing, and any survival percentage.

## 5. Weakest line

b2, "The pup can't swim yet." No read source says it in those words; it is the only line resting
on inference. Two options: keep it (the six-week fast before feeding makes it near-certain, and it is
the hinge the story needs), or soften to **"The pup can't feed itself yet."**, which NOAA states
outright. I recommend the softer line; it costs nothing the story needs.
