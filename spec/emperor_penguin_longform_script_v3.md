# Why This Penguin Looks Like the Worst Mother

Hook: Why does an emperor penguin mother hand her only egg to Dad and vanish toward the sea?

1. [setup] (30w) Why does this emperor penguin look like the worst mother? She leaves her only egg with Dad and heads to sea. But how does a chick get fed at all?

2. [setup] (31w) The timing is fixed. Between May and June, she produces that single egg. One egg. Not a clutch of many, but one careful, precious try at a chick this whole season.

3. [setup] (29w) With the egg laid, she turns from the colony. She heads back toward the sea to feed. So down toward the water she goes, leaving the ice behind her.

4. [hinge] (10w) There is no nest, yet the egg must stay warm.

5. [mechanism] (35w) So he keeps the egg warm beneath a fold of skin. There, it stays warm at about thirty-eight degrees, while outside the temperature can fall well below minus thirty-five. Two worlds, one thin layer apart.

6. [mechanism] (27w) Hold that gap in your head. Inside, blood-warm shelter. Outside, an extreme cold. And the only thing bridging the two is one father, keeping the temperature steady.

7. [escalation] (39w) This is not a quick shift. He holds the egg on his feet, under that brood pouch, for something close to two months — roughly sixty-two to sixty-seven days. Day after day, the same posture, the same guarded warmth.

8. [escalation] (23w) The egg cannot follow. So he stays put, task fixed to one spot, holding the egg on his feet under that brood pouch.

9. [escalation] (28w) Through all this time, he eats nothing at all. He runs entirely on the fat stored in his body, and his frame shrinks as the days grind on.

10. [escalation] (29w) By the end, some fathers have lost close to half of what they weighed. Picture that. Half a body, gone — all while fasting on nothing but stored fat.

11. [escalation] (32w) Now suppose the chick cracks free of its shell before the mother has come back from the sea. Hungry, and needing food, not only warmth. And the father hasn't eaten in weeks.

12. [escalation] (37w) So how does an empty father feed a newborn? Here is the answer. He can feed the chick by regurgitation before the female returns, despite not having eaten for weeks. Food from a body that hasn't fished.

13. [reversal] (34w) Out of the ice, the mother returns, fat from the sea. But the colony is thousands of birds deep. So she calls. And somewhere in that wall of sound, one voice calls back. His.

14. [reversal] (19w) They find each other. From her full belly she brings up fish and krill, feeding the chick at last.

15. [verdict] (33w) So watch that moment again — not the leaving, but this. The mother, back on the ice, feeding a chick that is warm, alive, and full. Everything the pair did was aimed here.

16. [verdict] (18w) She didn't leave the egg unprotected. She left it with its father — and came back with dinner.
